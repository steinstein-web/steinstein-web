# 🌍 Featured Product

## 🧭 PathwayPAL

> **A smarter way to plan a journey from one place.**

**PathwayPAL** is a travel planning and itinerary management platform designed to reduce the fragmentation involved in planning trips.

Instead of jumping between multiple platforms for transport, accommodation, activities, food and itinerary planning, PathwayPAL brings the planning process into one structured experience.

### What it focuses on

`Destination Exploration` · `Transport Comparison` · `Accommodation Discovery` · `Activities` · `Dining` · `Itinerary Management`

### Product direction

**V1 → Discover → Compare → Plan → Organize**

Future development can extend the platform toward bookings, payments, live availability, tourism experiences and other travel services.

🔗 **Repository:**
https://github.com/steinstein-web/PathwayPal
